# Independent operator and domain acceptance protocol

Status: **0 independent participants; no completed domain acceptance.** All recorded engineering review actions were operated by Codex. Test usernames and simulated preparer/approver do not count as two human reviewers.

Recruit at least three people unfamiliar with BatchFence, including a quality/traceability professional. Use synthetic or owner-approved de-identified records, counterbalanced equivalent manual and assisted cases, and a fixed instruction sheet. No recruitment messages have been sent.

1. Record role/experience, consent, dataset variant, order and timestamp. Include CSV mapping, procedure approval and source preparation time in both methods.
2. Ask each participant to find the affected stock, explain an ambiguous parent, review a revised notice, and state proposed versus currently applied units without coaching.
3. Ask them to show why 800 held remains 800 when the proposal falls to 700; obtain the separate policy/evidence/distinct approval and recover exactly 100. Include an overlapping incident hold and a stale approval challenge.
4. Ask them to distinguish a statistical signal from a defect specification or causal conclusion. Freeze a defensible baseline and decide whether insufficient effectiveness data supports closure.
5. Record task completion time, errors, raw AI omissions/false candidates, correction count/time, unnecessary holds, unsafe exclusions, requests for help, perceived workload, preparation/integration effort and refusal to trust the result. Stop if real inventory or confidential data is involved.
6. Repeat with a meaningfully different notice and incomplete/contradictory evidence. Keep per-participant raw observations, medians/ranges and denominators. Report unsuccessful runs and learning/order effects. Three users are exploratory evidence, not a population estimate.

The domain reviewer must approve the chosen nonconforming-unit metric, subgroup independence/comparability, baseline policy, whole-pool recovery authenticity/inspection rules, accepted procedures, effectiveness window/criteria, data completeness and authority model. A real organization must provision distinct identities, approve external AMD processing, name operating/incident owners, set RPO/RTO and pass an off-device restore. Actual enterprise integration stays prohibited until separately authorized.

Value worksheet (currently unmeasured): net time benefit = manual total minutes minus assisted total minutes including onboarding/corrections; carrying value released is not savings. Report labor cost only with the owner's actual loaded rate. Include integration/maintenance cost and the possibility of negative benefit. No customer validation or ROI is currently established.
